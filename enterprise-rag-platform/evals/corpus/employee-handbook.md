# Northwind Robotics Employee Handbook

This handbook applies to all full-time employees of Northwind Robotics. Contractors are
governed by their statement of work.

## Time Off

### Vacation

New employees receive 20 vacation days per year, accrued monthly from the first day of
employment. After five years of service the allowance increases to 25 vacation days per
year. Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over into the next calendar year.

### Parental Leave

Northwind offers 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave to all parents, including adoptive
and foster parents. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year.

### Sick Leave

Sick leave is unlimited for short illnesses. Absences longer than five consecutive working
days require a note from a medical professional.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with their manager's agreement. Fully
remote arrangements require approval from a vice president and are reviewed every year.
Core collaboration hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the employee's home office time zone.

## Learning Budget

Every employee has an annual learning budget of 1,500 USD for courses, books and
conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.
