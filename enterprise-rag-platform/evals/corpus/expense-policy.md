# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals

The meal per diem is 75 USD per day for domestic travel and 100 USD per day for
international travel. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved for client events.

## Receipts and Submission

Itemized receipts are required for any expense above 25 USD. Expense reports must be
submitted in the SpendPoint tool within 30 days of the expense date; late reports require
director approval.

## Flights

Economy class is the default for all flights. Business class may be booked only for
flights longer than 8 hours of scheduled flying time. Flights must be booked at least
14 days in advance whenever possible.

## Lodging

Hotel bookings should not exceed 250 USD per night in major cities and 180 USD per night
elsewhere, excluding taxes.
