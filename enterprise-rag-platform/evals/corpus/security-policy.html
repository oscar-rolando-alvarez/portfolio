Information Security Policy

Passwords and Authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, source control and the VPN.

Devices

All company laptops must use full-disk encryption and lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Lost or stolen devices must be reported within 1 hour.

Data Classification

Northwind classifies information into four levels:

  • Public: approved for external release.
  • Internal: default level for business information.
  • Confidential: customer data and unreleased financials.
  • Restricted: security keys, credentials and regulated personal data.

Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported in the sec-incidents channel within 1 hour of discovery. Do not attempt to investigate an incident on your own.
