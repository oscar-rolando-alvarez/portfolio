# IT Support Guide

## VPN Access

Install the NorthVPN client from the self-service software center and sign in with your
company account. Multi-factor authentication is required on every connection.

## Password Resets

Forgotten passwords are reset through the self-service identity portal. The help desk
never asks for your password.

## Hardware Lifecycle

Laptops are replaced every 3 years, or earlier when a hardware fault cannot be repaired.
Monitors and docking stations are replaced every 5 years.
